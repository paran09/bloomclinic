When to get help

Contact your maternity care team or seek urgent medical care if you have warning signs such as:

  • Vaginal bleeding or severe abdominal pain
  • Severe headache, blurred vision, or sudden swelling
  • High fever, difficulty breathing, or feeling seriously unwell
  • Your baby’s movements slow or change once you usually feel them

If symptoms feel severe or life-threatening, seek emergency care now. Do not wait.
